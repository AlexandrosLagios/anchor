Anchor · Demo script

About 2½ minutes. A daughter shares a moment, her father says yes himself, the moment comes back to him, he gets gentle help, and he answers by voice.

Cast

Elenithe daughter · family group Nikosthe grandfather · his private chat Presenternarrates · shows both screens

Just before you start

  • Eleni's phone shows the family group. Nikos's phone shows his chat with Anchor.
  • Eleni has the first-day-of-school photo ready. Nikos has his 1958 photo ready.
  • The technical checks are done (see the technical runbook).

The script

0:00Eleni
Post the photo with the caption "Maria's first day of school! She wore her new red backpack."
They see: Anchor reacts with ❤. It kept the moment in Eleni's own words, with no extra step.
0:25Eleni
Reply /private to Nikos's message in the group.
They see: "Nikos, the family would love your stories 💛" and a Start button.
0:40Nikos
Tap Start. Read the welcome aloud. Tap "Yes, I'd like that".
They see: Anchor says it is not a person, there is no right answer, it shares nothing without a yes, and /stop ends it. He decides himself.
1:05Eleni
Send /send in the group.
They see: Nikos gets the photo and a voice note: "Eleni shared: «Maria's first day of school!…» What does it remind you of?"
1:25Nikos
Reply "a school?"
They see: "No rush 🙂 … Any memory it brings is welcome." No correction, and nothing shows in the family group.
Presenter: "He hesitated, and nobody else saw it."
1:45Nikos
Send a voice note: "My first day was in 1958. My mother walked me to the village school, and I cried at the gate."
They see: "Thank you for the story 💛 Shall I share it with the family?"
2:10Nikos
Tap "Yes, share it".
They see: In the family group, "Nikos added a story to Eleni's moment 🎙️" with his words and his voice.
2:30Presenter
"Eleni shared a moment. Her father agreed himself, got help without a correction, and added his story by voice."

Extra, only if you are under 2:30: Nikos posts his 1958 photo in the group with "My first day of school, 1958", and Anchor shows "Then and now".

If something goes wrong

No ❤ after 15 seconds: keep talking and go on to the next beat.

Nothing reaches Nikos after /send: check that he tapped "Yes, I'd like that", then send /send again.

"a school?" gets a different answer: Eleni sends /send again, and Nikos goes on with the voice note.

Two beats fail: stop and play the recording of the best rehearsal.

Rehearse twice with a stopwatch. Keep the whole script under 3 minutes.
