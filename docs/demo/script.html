Anchor · Demo script

About 3 minutes of a 6-minute presentation. No commands: every member chooses what Anchor sends, a share offer, a reminder offer, and voice both ways. The phone call is the stretch goal.

Cast

Elenithe daughter · group admin · the family group Nikosthe grandfather · the family group, then his private chat Presenternarrates · shows both phones side by side

Just before you start

  • Eleni's phone shows the family group throughout. Nikos's phone shows the family group, then his private chat with Anchor.
  • Eleni has the staged photo of a child's first day at school ready, with its caption.
  • For beat 7, Nikos's phone is ready to answer on speaker, and it has the "Anchor" contact saved, because Anchor calls from a US number.
  • The technical checks are done (see the technical runbook).

The script

0:00Nikos
In the group, write "Anchor, can you send me the family photos?"
The audience sees: Only Nikos sees Anchor's answer: "Nikos, I can send you family moments, reminders, and voice notes in private. Tap to choose 🙂", with the button "Choose what I send you". Eleni's screen shows only his question. No command, and no admin acts for him.
0:20Nikos
Tap "Choose what I send you", then Start. Tap "Family moments now and then" and "Talk to me by voice", then "Done".
The audience sees: Anchor says in plain words that it is not a person, and each choice turns ✅ in place. After "Done", Anchor answers with a voice note: "All set 💛". Every member chooses for themselves, and nobody needs to type or read.
0:50Eleni
Post the photo with the caption "Maria's first day of school! She wore her new red backpack."
The audience sees: Anchor reacts with ❤. Only Eleni sees "Shall I send this to Nikos now?". She taps "Yes, send it", and the offer turns into "Sent to Nikos 💛".
1:10Nikos
In private, play the voice note. Answer with a voice note of about 10 seconds: "My first day was in 1958. My mother walked me to the village school, and I cried at the gate." Tap "Yes, share it".
The audience sees: Nikos gets the photo, then a voice note: "Eleni shared: «Maria's first day of school! …» What does it remind you of?". Anchor thanks him by voice. In the group: "Nikos added a story to Eleni's moment 🎙️", with his words, his voice note, and a big ❤.
1:50Eleni
In the group, reply to Nikos's message of beat 1 with "Dad, remember to take your pills with you when we leave in the morning."
The audience sees: Only Nikos sees "⏰ Eleni wrote: «Dad, remember to take your pills…» Shall I remind you?", with "Yes, at 08:00", "Another time", "No thanks", and "Stop offering reminders". He taps "Yes, at 08:00". The offer turns into "Done ✍ I'll remind you at 08:00 in our private chat.", and Eleni's message gets a ✍. The family sees only the ✍: the pills stay private.
2:15Eleni
Send /fastforward 08:05 in the group.
The audience sees: Only Eleni sees "⏩ It's now … 08:05 on the family clock." A few seconds later, Nikos gets his reminder in private as a voice note: "⏰ Your reminder. Eleni wrote: «…»".
2:35Nikos
Stretch goal. Run this beat only when the call gate passed and the clock shows 2:35 or less. In private, say "Call me" in a voice note. Answer the phone on speaker. Answer Anchor in one sentence, say "No, thanks" to the share question, and "Yes" to the question about Eleni.
The audience sees: Nikos's phone rings. Anchor opens with "Hello Nikos, this is Anchor, the family's record keeper. I'm not a person." and reads Eleni's moment in her words. Nikos answers in one sentence. Anchor asks "Shall I tell Eleni you'd love a call?". He says yes, and the group gets "Eleni, Nikos would love a call from you 💛".
2:40 or 3:15Presenter
"No commands. Nikos chose what he gets, heard Eleni's moment in her words, added his story by voice, and got his reminder in private. The family saw a ❤ and a ✍."
The audience sees: Both screens stay on the group.

If a beat fails

No answer for Nikos in beat 1 after 10 seconds: Nikos writes "Anchor, settings". The same button arrives.

No share offer for Eleni in beat 3 after 15 seconds: check that Nikos turned on "Family moments" in beat 2. Nikos then says "Send me a moment" to Anchor in private, and the photo arrives.

"Thank you 💛" instead of the share question in beat 4: Anchor closed the invitation. Nikos says "Send me a moment" in private, and repeats beat 4.

No reminder offer for Nikos in beat 5 after 10 seconds: Eleni writes "Dad, don't forget your pills tomorrow at 8." With no offer again, narrate beats 5 and 6, and continue with beat 7 or 8.

No reminder after /fastforward in beat 6 after 10 seconds: narrate the reminder, and continue.

"I couldn't ring you just now" in beat 7, or no ring after 15 seconds: say "The phone call is our next step," and close with beat 8.

The call rings, but Anchor stays silent for 5 seconds: Nikos hangs up. Say "The phone call is our next step," and close with beat 8.

Two beats fail: stop the live demo and play the screen recording.

Rehearse twice with a stopwatch. Keep the script under 3 minutes without beat 7.
